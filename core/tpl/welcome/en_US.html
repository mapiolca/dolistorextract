Thank you for choosing one of my Dolibarr modules. I hope it will save you time, simplify your everyday work and meet your needs.

Updates when they are useful to you

From time to time, you will receive emails about your module to let you know about:

  • new versions available;
  • features that enhance the module;
  • important fixes;
  • tips and advice to help you get the most out of it.

I prefer to contact you only when I have something truly useful to share.

Your feedback helps the modules evolve

I place great importance on feedback from my users. If you encounter a bug, have an idea for an improvement or need a feature, please let me know.

Many improvements to my modules are directly inspired by the needs their users express. Every suggestion is considered carefully, even though I cannot guarantee that it will be implemented immediately.

My goal is simple: to improve these modules based on real working practices, so that they become increasingly practical, efficient and pleasant to use.

YOUR PURCHASE, CLEARLY EXPLAINED

One year of free updates

Your purchase entitles you to one year of free updates, starting on the date of your DoliStore purchase. During this period, you can download every new version released at no additional cost.

A permanent licence

Your licence is permanent. Once the update period has ended, the module will continue to work normally.

Only access to new versions will require renewal of the update period if you wish to continue benefiting from the latest improvements.

I develop these modules by staying close to their users. Your feedback directly helps define priorities for upcoming versions. Together, we can improve them to meet the needs of the Dolibarr community even better.

Thank you again for your trust, and welcome to the community of users of my modules.
